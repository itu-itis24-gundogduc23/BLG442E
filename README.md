# ⛏️ Gold Diggers: AI-Driven Mining Feasibility

> **Disclaimer:** This repository contains the prototype and conceptual framework for **Gold Diggers**, an innovative project developed by **Team Innovax**. The platform is currently in the prototype stage and serves as a demonstration of our vision for transforming mining feasibility studies through artificial intelligence.

---

## 🌍 Vision

Mining projects involve substantial uncertainty, long evaluation cycles, and significant financial risk. Traditional pre-feasibility studies often require extensive drilling campaigns, large budgets, and months—or even years—of analysis before a project's viability becomes clear.

**Gold Diggers** aims to bridge the gap between raw geological data and investment decisions by providing a fast, cost-effective, and probabilistically driven decision-support platform for mining companies.

---

## 🛑 The Challenge

Traditional mining feasibility assessments face several critical limitations:

- **High Costs & Long Timelines**  
  Drilling campaigns and geological surveys are expensive and time-consuming, often requiring millions of dollars before meaningful conclusions can be reached.

- **Deterministic Evaluations**  
  Most existing tools provide a single projected outcome, making it difficult to understand the true range of financial risk.

- **Investment Uncertainty**  
  Significant capital is frequently invested in projects that ultimately fail to achieve positive economic returns.

---

## 💡 Our Approach

Gold Diggers combines artificial intelligence with established geostatistical techniques to support more informed decision-making.

### Key Features

- **Probabilistic NPV Analysis**  
  Generate a range of potential Net Present Value (NPV) outcomes instead of a single deterministic estimate.

- **Reduced Drilling Requirements**  
  Leverage historical drilling records and regional geological data to reduce exploration effort.

- **AI-Integrated Drilling Optimization**  
  Utilize a conceptual AI-assisted hexagonal drilling pattern designed to improve sampling efficiency and resource estimation accuracy.

- **Rapid Project Screening**  
  Compress evaluation timelines from months to days, enabling faster investment decisions.
